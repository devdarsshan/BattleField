# Battle of the Hydaspes (326 BC): Shot List

One row per script beat (41 beats). Built for the current pipeline: **Flow reference stills (free) > user approval > Blender build to match > voice > render > edit** (`../_channel-context/Production_Playbook.md`, `Blender_Build_Rules.md`).

- **Estimated length: 12:29** including the 18 s end screen (limit 10 to 13 minutes). Each beat is its narration at about 132 words a minute plus about 1.8 s, minimum 10 s. The real Algieba takes will re-time everything.
- **Hook check:** S01 is on screen at 0:00 with the strongest image (the line of war elephants at the flooded river, Porus on the biggest, Alexander's camp across the water). "So, who really won?" lands inside the first 30 s. The **title card (under 2 s) goes between S02 and S03**.
- **Cuts:** continuous camera within a set; a clean cut or cloud dissolve when the place or scale changes (Join column).
- **No text** in any still or render. Names, dates, quotes, source names (Arrian, Curtius, Plutarch, Diodorus) and map labels are added in the edit. When the narration names an ancient writer, the edit shows a small name plate with the writer's century ("Arrian, about 450 years later"), so viewers can see how late the sources are.
- **Facts:** every picture matches the script and `Sources.md`. Where the sources disagree (numbers, the double, Bucephalus), show the event plainly and let the narration carry the hedge. Never show a number on screen that the script does not give.

## Look and colours
- Same channel look as Colachel and Saraighat: low-poly, flat-shaded, matte, bounded haze box, AgX Medium Low Contrast (outdoor) / Base Contrast (map, interiors).
- **Porus's side = gold** (white cotton with gold and saffron; gold on the map). **Alexander's side = blue** (blue cloaks and shield faces, bronze helmets; blue on the map). Taxila = pale tan on the map. The Mauryas (S40) = purple.
- Punjab in the monsoon: a wide brown flooded river, grey rain light for S01-S16, sun breaking through for the battle, warm evening for the meeting. Flat plain, scattered trees, tall grass, mud; snowy mountains far to the north on clear shots.

## Accuracy notes for the stills (Flow tends to get these wrong)
- **Elephants carry no towers.** Riders sit on the elephant's back: a driver on the neck and one or two fighters behind. Towers (howdahs) belong to later centuries.
- **Indian infantry:** white cotton, long bows (taller than a man, braced on the ground to shoot), broad two-handed swords, light shields; turbans or bare heads. No metal armour for the rank and file.
- **Indian chariots:** heavy, drawn by four horses, several men on board.
- **Macedonian phalanx:** very long pikes (sarissas, about 6 m), small round shields hung from the shoulder, bronze helmets, linen body armour. **Companion cavalry:** no stirrups, short lances, cloaks.
- **Alexander:** about 29, clean-shaven, wavy shoulder-length hair, bronze cuirass, blue cloak, plumed helmet in battle. **Bucephalus:** big black horse with a white star on the forehead.
- **Porus:** very tall (the Greeks say well over six feet), dark beard, white and gold robes, a light corselet in battle (his right shoulder is uncovered: S26), gold ornaments.
- **No gore.** The elephant charge (S22) and the stampede (S24) show bodies knocked back and men scattering, dust, fallen standards. No blood, no crushed figures.

## New kit parts (build once, before any scene)
1. **War elephant** with driver and one or two riders (no tower); idle sway, walk, charge and rearing animation. The single most important asset: the Indian audience cares most about the elephants.
2. **Horse and rider** (Companion cavalry, Indian horsemen, Bucephalus variant) if the kit has none.
3. **Four-horse chariot** with crew.
4. **Phalanx block:** a formation of pikemen with long sarissas that can lower and advance together.
5. **Indian infantry:** archers with long bows, swordsmen; blocks for the line.
6. **River water:** the faceted water in flood brown, fast flow, rain rings; lightning and thunderstorm lighting for E.
7. **Macedonian camp:** tents, grain-sack piles, carts, horse lines, campfires (reuse kit tents, sacks, carts where possible).
8. **Stone altar** (tall, plain) for the Beas.
