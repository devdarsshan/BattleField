## Points flagged as uncertain in the script
| Point | What the sources say | How the script handles it |
|---|---|---|
| Date of surrender | 7 Aug (Dutch records, Ibrahim Kunju), 10 Aug (Panikkar, traditional), 31 Jul (Menon) | Gives 7 Aug from Dutch records, 10 Aug as remembered, and says historians argue |
| Size of the Dutch force | No reliable total. Reinforcements of 105 + 70; one popular article says 400 | "Perhaps a few hundred", with "the records do not give a clear total" |
| Size of the Travancore army | 2,000 Nair levy confirmed; "over 10,000" in one popular article | Only the 2,000 levy is given |
| The king's "invade Europe" reply | Reported by M. O. Koshy; wording varies | Attributed to Koshy and flagged as possibly grown in the telling |
| Temple visit at Thiruvattar | Legend | "Legend says" |
| Fishermen's fake palm cannons | Folklore | "No record from the time proves it" |
| Fishermen's help (bribes refused, misleading troops) | De Lannoy's own account | Attributed to De Lannoy |
| Prisoner count | 24 or 28 | "About twenty-four" |
| Surrender terms broken | "Reportedly" | "Some historians say" |
| "First Asian win over a European power" | Popular claim, but untrue (Mughals at Hooghly 1632, Koxinga in Taiwan 1662) | The script corrects it |
| De Lannoy's death | Tombstone says 1777, served about 37 years | 1777, about 37 years |
| Kingdom Imhoff demanded back | Elayadathu Swaroopam (Kottarakkara) in one source, Kayamkulam in another | "One of the kingdoms he has taken" |
