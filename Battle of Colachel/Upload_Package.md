WHERE HISTORIANS DISAGREE
We say so in the narration instead of picking a side quietly. In this video:
- The surrender date: 7 August (Dutch records) or 10 August (as remembered in Kerala).
- The size of the Dutch force: the records do not give a clear total.
- The king's famous reply to Van Imhoff may have grown in the telling.
- The story of the fishermen's fake palm-trunk cannons is folklore, with no record from the time.
- "The first time an Asian kingdom beat a European power" is a popular claim, but it is too big. We explain why.
