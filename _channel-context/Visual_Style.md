# Visual style for Flow generations

Reference images are in `../style-refs/`. Use them for the look only. Never recreate a specific frame from them.

## Look
- Low-poly 3D, flat-shaded, faceted geometry. Blocky figures: box bodies, faceted heads, minimal faces, no realistic detail.
- Matte materials, soft warm daylight, long soft shadows, slight haze. A muted earthy palette, with each side in one strong colour (for Colachel, Dutch dark blue against Travancore white-and-red).
- Low-poly faceted palms, trees and terrain. Simple cone tents and plain props.
- Wide or medium-wide framing from a slightly high angle, like a diorama.

## The world as a diorama on a map
- The whole film takes place in one 3D diorama standing on aged parchment, with faint grid lines and a blue sea and rivers.
- Seen from straight above, it reads as a map with small 3D trees, forts and armies standing on the paper.
- To change place, the camera rises to map height, glides across, and comes back down. This is how a film with no cuts can still move between locations.

## Text (added in the edit, never generated)
- Name plates: a dark plaque with a thin gold border, the name in serif capitals, and a one-line italic gloss underneath.
- Title card: a dark brown or charcoal map silhouette. "THE BATTLE OF" in small spaced capitals above a large bevelled gold serif title, then a line of sides and date, with thin gold rules and a small red star.
- Map labels: small engraved serif italics.
- Always add "no text, no letters, no watermark" to Flow prompts. Text generated by AI comes out garbled.
