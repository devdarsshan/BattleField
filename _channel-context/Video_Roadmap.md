# Video Roadmap: which battle comes next

Built 2026-10-21 from the user's list of 15 candidates, plus extra battles found in research. Scored against `Growth_and_Scripting_Insights.md` (proven idea, relatability, conflict arc, open-loop hook, brand fit) and checked against YouTube search results the same day.

**Lesson from video 1:** Colachel got 23 views and about 15 s per view in its first 2 days. The topic had little proven demand in English long-form, and the opening did not hook. So the order below puts **proven demand with little English long-form competition** first, and every script still has to follow the 15-second hook rule.

## How each battle is scored
Each factor is scored 0-5. **Total = Demand×2 + Gap×2 + Reach + Story + Safety + Build** (max 40). Ties go to higher Reach.

| Factor | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Demand** | Proven views on YouTube: long-form videos and Shorts, in any language |
| **Gap** | Room for a new channel: few strong English long-form videos, no big channel owning it (0 = saturated, or HistoryBattles3D / Epic History already did it in 3D) |
| **Reach** | Appeal to both the US and India |
| **Story** | Strength of the conflict arc: underdog, betrayal, twist, comeback |
| **Safety** | Low political or communal risk in comments (5 = safe) |
| **Build** | How well it fits the low-poly diorama style and reuses `colachel_kit.py` (18th-century Europeans, ships, coast, forts) |

## Batches
- **Batch A (next 4): Saraighat, Hydaspes, Sivaganga, Pollilur.** Strongest demand-to-competition ratio. Two are Indian victories over Europeans (the Colachel theme), and one is the strongest US + India crossover topic.
- **Batch B: Plassey, Myeongnyang, Assaye, Pratapgad.** Proven stories. Plassey and Assaye reuse almost the whole Colachel kit. Pratapgad has huge demand but high communal risk, so it waits until the channel has some audience.
- **Batch C: Talikota, Isandlwana + Rorke's Drift, Saragarhi, Rezang La / Longewala.** Bigger demand but more competition, or a jump to modern armour that needs a new kit.
- **Parked:** Thermopylae, Hastings, Agincourt and Gaugamela are owned by channels with 1-37M views, and HistoryBattles3D already has 3D videos of Hastings (2.07M) and Agincourt (3.35M). Vasai, Adyar and Buxar have little story demand outside exam-prep channels. Haldighati is crowded and politically heated.

## Why each top pick
- **Saraighat 1671.** Shorts on Lachit Borphukan reach 1.7M-2.8M, but the best English long-form is about 180K, with no big channel. In July 2026 the Assam CM announced a Hindi Lachit biopic, so search interest should rise. Arc: a sick commander, an imperial army, and a river-boat battle on the Brahmaputra; a moving-water diorama makes a strong hook visual. Risk: told as Ahom vs Mughal, keep it about the river and the man, not religion.
- **Hydaspes 326 BCE.** The biggest US + India crossover topic. English long-form is old (BazBattles 1.65M, 9 years; Kings and Generals ~500K, 3 years), while Shorts reach 8.2M ("it's all a lie"). Open-loop hook: "who really won?" Most historians credit Alexander with the battle, and Porus kept his kingdom. Telling it honestly will draw comments both ways; hedge it, and cite sources. Needs new assets: phalanx, elephants, a river in a storm.
- **Sivaganga 1780 (Velu Nachiyar).** Tamil videos reach 1M+ long-form and an 8.2M Short, but English coverage is close to empty (the biggest about 52K). A woman lead, years in exile, and taking her kingdom back with Hyder Ali's help, against the Nawab of Arcot and Company troops. Same era and south-India setting as Colachel. Kuyili's suicide attack on the armoury is debated, so hedge it ("Tradition says…").
- **Pollilur 1780.** The biggest gap found. English long-form tops out in the tens of thousands (Tiger of Mysore channel 7K-77K depending on the search region; Kings and Generals covers it only inside a broader Tipu video at 228K), while a "rockets against the British" Short has 1.6M. Hook: a rocket hits the ammunition carts. Reuses the Colachel kit (redcoats, cannon, camp). Risk: Tipu is politically polarising in India; keep to the battle, not the man's legacy.

## Rules carried into every video
1. Cut a 45-60 s Short from each long video (`make_short.py`). For Indian topics most of the demand is in Shorts.
2. Titles that win use a **number contrast** ("13 vs 133", "21 vs 10,000") or a **myth-buster** ("Did Porus really…"). Use only numbers that hold up in `Sources.md`.
3. Before writing each script, do the competitor breakdown of its top 3 videos (Growth file, section 11.6), starting from the videos named above.
4. Re-score the next batch after each upload, using its 48-hour numbers from `PROGRESS.md`.
